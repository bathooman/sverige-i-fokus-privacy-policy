    </p>
  </section>

  <section class="privacy-section">
    <h2>Data stored on your device</h2>
    <p>The app stores the following data exclusively on your device using a local SQLite database:</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Reading progress, such as which sections you have read</li>
      <li>Flashcard ratings, such as known or review</li>
      <li>Quiz and mock test results</li>
      <li>App preferences, such as notification settings</li>
    </ul>
    <p>
      This data is not transmitted to a server operated by us. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or use the "Reset all progress" option in Settings.
    </p>
  </section>

  <section class="privacy-section">

  </section>
